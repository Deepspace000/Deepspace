about

deepspace is the ambient project of Mirko Ruckels, an Australian musician living in Brisbane.

Since 2006 he has made slow, spacious music from synthesizers, software and acoustic instruments. Many pieces picture places like empty rooms, night cities, deep water and far planets.

His albums are released independently and on Projekt Records.
